# Contributing

This repository is a research artifact with frozen evidence boundaries.

Before proposing a change:

1. identify whether the file is scientific evidence, publication exposition, executable research tooling or future architecture;
2. do not rewrite frozen evidence;
3. do not strengthen an empirical claim without new independently frozen evidence;
4. keep negative/null/partial results intact;
5. do not add secrets, auth material or private enterprise data;
6. treat execution scripts as historical research tooling, not standing authority.

Publication-layer clarifications may improve wording, structure, citations and figures without modifying underlying experimental results.
