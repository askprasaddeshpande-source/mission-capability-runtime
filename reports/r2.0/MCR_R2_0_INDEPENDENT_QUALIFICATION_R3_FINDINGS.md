## What held
Review 2's tampered-delegation attack and the other normal-path gate attacks were refused (664 hash-invalid, wrong-kind and non-dict variants refused at every gate with state unchanged). Atomic rollback held under injected failures, KeyboardInterrupt and TypeError. The 14-link order matched; `COMPLETE` re-checked the whole chain (R01-R49 plus the predicate check); about 3,700 consistent-hash forgeries reached COMPLETE only through ids, the documented unenforced fields, a wider delegation scope, weakened predicates and extra mutation ids. Frozen evidence was byte-identical at all four commits (64 pins correct); the 13/14 live result was preserved accurately and rebuilt from the freeze; the frozen runner reproduced 6/6 mock scenarios; no token, JWT or private key in the tree or any ref (243 blobs including nested zips and UTF-16). The verifier passed plain and with the external anchor.

## What failed (public API)
1. **F1 HIGH:** `resume()` of a pre-send lease reached EXECUTION_STARTED (a send) on hash-valid evidence the normal gates refuse: unfrozen outcome contract, budget retries and expansion, non-single-use authority, wider authority scope, mismatched capability, and no delegation or capability at all. The agent reproduced this independently (a budget with retries=1 reached EXECUTION_STARTED through resume).
2. **F2 HIGH:** the replay ledger had no file locking and no per-authority send count. Two real processes sharing one ledger file both sent the same mutation (reproduced independently); two missions sharing one single-use authority sent two different mutation ids on a budget of 1; a fresh or absent ledger allowed a second send; torn or truncated lines and a CR in a mutation id corrupted the keys.
3. **F3 MEDIUM:** COMPENSATED was reachable with zero compensating action by re-presenting the forward readback as the post-compensation readback (reproduced independently); preconditions were evaluated on a non-authoritative forward readback.
4. **F4 LOW-MEDIUM:** a truthy non-True `frozen` and NaN `max_mutations` passed the gates but failed at COMPLETE; booleans and floats were accepted as budget numbers.
5. **F5 LOW:** a readback with `existence=EXISTS` and `observed.exists=False` reached COMPLETE if the predicates omitted `exists`; malformed types raised TypeError instead of GuardError (state unchanged).
6. **F6 LOW:** verifier gaps: JSON-quoted-key secrets, `.yaml` and extension-less files, base64 Bearer values, unquoted `password=`, `x-api-key`, Basic auth, UTF-16 files, UNC share paths and profile environment-variable paths passed without the anchor; `*.pyc` and `__pycache__` passed even with the anchor; an empty `--expect-sums-sha256 ""` printed PASS with the anchor unchecked.
7. **F7 coverage:** the repo's 159 mutants reproduced with 0 survivors, but only at whole-condition granularity. The reviewer's 378 clause-level operator mutants gave 334 killed and 44 survived (about half equivalent or redundant); real gaps included `<=` to `<` in `attenuated` and R06 (equal scope is what the live freeze uses), single clauses in `resume`, R09 and R20, the `validate_outcome` contract-hash clause, and hand mutants for ledger append-to-truncate, removing `fsync`, removing `flush`, and "first UNKNOWN hides a later FAIL".
